Segment Impact Profiler

Three interactive approaches to SHAP explanations across population groups. All charts are self-contained, work offline, and use a synthetic LightGBM credit-risk model.

01 — Feature Impact FlowDirectional SHAP component magnitudes through a three-layer Sankey. Ribbons are not customer flows. 02 — Comparative Impact AtlasSmall-multiple signed lollipop plots, with circle area reflecting overall feature strength. 02b — Comparative Atlas, feature-focusedSame view, temporarily excluding the dominant Age feature to surface other predictors. 04 — Segment Diagnostic DashboardChoose a population group; inspect signed effects, differential effects, raw-margin waterfall, and mean PD.

Source code and methods are documented in README.md in the accompanying package. Plots are illustrative; SHAP describes model predictions, not causal effects. All contributions use a raw-margin output scale.
